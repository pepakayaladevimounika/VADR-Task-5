## SECTION 5: CLINICAL RECOMMENDATIONS & MEDICAL REVIEW
1. **Absolute Cessation:** Permanently discontinue Atorvastatin. Mark all HMG-CoA reductase inhibitors (statins) as high-alert warnings in the patient's electronic health records.
2. **Acute Management:** Continue intensive intravenous fluid resuscitation with isotonic saline to maintain high urine output and promote urinary alkalinization, protecting renal filtration pathways.
3. **Alternative Lipid-Lowering Regimen:** Once clinical renal markers settle back to baseline, evaluate alternative non-statin lipid therapies such as **Ezetimibe** or **PCSK9 Inhibitors (e.g., Evolocumab)** to manage cardiovascular hyperlipidemia risks without triggering skeletal muscle toxicities.
